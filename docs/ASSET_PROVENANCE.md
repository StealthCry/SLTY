# Asset provenance and usage notes

## Temporary weapon / hands concept references

The following files are stored in `assets/references/concept-examples/`:

- `weapon-hands-concept.webp`
- `weapon-model-preview.webp`
- `weapon-model-preview-alt.webp`

The project owner reports that these images were generated by them using an external AI image-generation tool. They are intentionally retained in the public repository as temporary visual examples of the desired in-game weapon and first-person-hands direction.

They are **not approved final assets**, not game-ready models, and must not be treated as finished production content. Use them as visual references when designing or commissioning the final weapon, hands, materials, rig, and animations.

## Rights and licensing

- The images are owner-generated AI references, not third-party stock images supplied by an unknown source.
- The terms of the external AI service may govern redistribution, modification, and commercial use. The project owner should retain the relevant generation records and verify those terms before shipping the images or derived assets in a public or commercial build.
- The root Apache License 2.0 applies to original project code the owner has the right to license; it does not automatically grant rights to image assets.
- Create or commission final production weapon assets separately and document their licenses before distribution.
