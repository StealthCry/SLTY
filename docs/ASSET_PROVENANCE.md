# Asset provenance and usage notes

## Temporary weapon / hands concept references

The following files are stored in `assets/references/concept-examples/`:

- `weapon-hands-concept.webp`
- `weapon-model-preview.webp`
- `weapon-model-preview-alt.webp`

They are temporary visual examples supplied to communicate the desired visual direction for the in-game weapon and first-person hands. They are **not approved final assets**, not game-ready models, and must not be treated as cleared production content.

## Rights and licensing

- The source/provenance and redistribution rights for each reference image have not yet been documented in this repository.
- Do not assume the root Apache License 2.0 grants rights to these images.
- Do not redistribute these images, publish them in a public build, or use them as shipped game content until their rights and terms have been verified.
- If the project owner created or otherwise has permission to use a reference, record the source, creator, license/permission, and any attribution requirements here.
- Create or commission final production weapon assets separately and document their licenses before distribution.

## Future asset record

For each production asset, record:
- File name and purpose
- Creator / source
- License or written permission
- Attribution / NOTICE requirements
- Whether modification and commercial distribution are allowed
- Date and evidence of verification
